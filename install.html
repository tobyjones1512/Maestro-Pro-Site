Install Maestro Pro

Maestro Pro needs a licence. Buy a licence and we will email you your key with this install link. Already paid? Get your licence key.

Maestro Pro is installed through Apple’s TestFlight app until it reaches the App Store.

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store on your iPhone, iPad or Mac.
  2. Open the Maestro Pro invitation and tap Accept, then Install.
  3. Open Maestro Pro. Tap “Already have a licence key?” on the paywall and enter your key.
