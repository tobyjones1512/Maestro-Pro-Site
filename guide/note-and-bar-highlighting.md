# Notes and bars that light up

Path: Guide > Start here > Playing a Score > Notes and bars that light up
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/note-and-bar-highlighting/
Summary: Notes glow as they sound and a soft wash marks the bar; this needs the Performer plan or above.

Notes glow as they sound, a soft wash marks the bar, and a moving line follows the music. This is part of the Performer and Professional plans: on the Free plan nothing follows along on the page. Choose the colour in [Highlight Colour](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/highlight-colour/).
