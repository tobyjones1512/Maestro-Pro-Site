# How to request a refund or cancel

Path: Guide > Plans and licences > Refunds and cancellation > How to request a refund or cancel
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/how-to-request-a-refund/
Summary: Use the cancellation page with your licence key and follow the steps.

1. Have your licence key ready. If you have lost it, [recover your licence key](https://maestro-pro.app/key.html) first.
2. Open the [cancellation page](https://maestro-pro.app/cancel/) and enter your key.
3. Check what the page says will happen. Nothing is cancelled or charged until you confirm.
4. You may be asked why you are cancelling, then confirm.

You receive a confirmation email saying the licence has been cancelled, any refund or fee, and the last day you can use the licence.
