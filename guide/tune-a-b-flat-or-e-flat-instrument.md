# Tune a B flat or E flat instrument

Path: Guide > Practise > Tuner & Wait for Me > Tune a B flat or E flat instrument
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/tune-a-b-flat-or-e-flat-instrument/
Summary: With your instrument set to B flat or E flat in the Inspector, the tuner shows the written note too.

If your instrument is set to B♭ or E♭ in the Inspector, the tuner shows the written note too, as well as the sounding note. Set the instrument first: see [Set your instrument](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/set-your-instrument/).
