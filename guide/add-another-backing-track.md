# Add more than one backing track

Path: Guide > Sound and words > Backing Tracks & Sound > Add more than one backing track
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/add-another-backing-track/
Summary: A score can have several tracks, for example a rhythm section and a separate guide vocal.

A score can have more than one backing track, for example a rhythm section and a separate guide vocal. Use **Add Another Backing Track** in the Inspector or the sound menu. They all play together, in time with the score.
