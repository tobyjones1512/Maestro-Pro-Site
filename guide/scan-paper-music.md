# Scanning paper music

Path: Guide > Start here > Your Library > Scanning paper music
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/scan-paper-music/
Summary: Scanning printed music with the camera is planned for a future update and is not in the app today.

Scanning paper music with the camera is coming in a future update. The app does not use the camera today. To use paper music now, scan it to a PDF with another app and import the PDF, and make sure the scan is straight and clear.
