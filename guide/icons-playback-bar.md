# Playback bar icons

Path: Guide > Settings and help > Buttons and icons > Playback bar icons
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/icons-playback-bar/
Summary: What each icon does: Go to Beginning, Previous Bar, Play, Next Bar and more.

- {{icon:backwardu2eendu2efill}} **Go to Beginning**: jumps back to the start of the score.
- {{icon:backwardu2efill}} **Previous Bar**: moves back one bar.
- {{icon:playu2efill}} **Play**: starts the music and the page follows along. It becomes a Pause button while playing.
- {{icon:forwardu2efill}} **Next Bar**: moves forward one bar.
- {{icon:metronome}} **Metronome**: switches the click on or off.
- {{icon:tuningfork}} **Key**: opens the key menu for a backing track or karaoke file, to move the music up or down in semitones.
- {{icon:chevronu2edownu2e2}} **Down a Tone**: in the key menu, lowers the music by two semitones.
- {{icon:chevronu2eupu2e2}} **Up a Tone**: in the key menu, raises the music by two semitones.
