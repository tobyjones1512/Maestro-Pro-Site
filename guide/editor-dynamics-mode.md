# Dynamics mode

Path: Guide > Write and keep > Writing & Editing Music > Dynamics mode
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/editor-dynamics-mode/
Summary: Put dynamics from ppp to fff on a note or bar, and crescendo or diminuendo hairpins over bars.

Tap a note (the dynamic goes at that note) or a bar (at its start), then choose ppp to fff. **Crescendo** and **Diminuendo** put a hairpin over the bar; **Longer** and **Shorter** stretch it over more bars.
