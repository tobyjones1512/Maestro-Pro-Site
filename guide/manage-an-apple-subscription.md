# Manage a subscription bought in the app

Path: Guide > Plans and licences > Plans and pricing > Manage a subscription bought in the app
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/manage-an-apple-subscription/
Summary: Performer and Professional bought in the app are managed in your Apple Account settings.

The paid plans bought inside the app are subscriptions, managed in your Apple Account settings. (If your plan comes from a licence key instead, Settings shows Manage Licence: see [Manage your licence in the app](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/manage-your-licence-in-the-app/).) That is where you change or stop them. To ask for a refund on a subscription bought in the app, see [Refunds for subscriptions bought in the app](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/refund-for-app-store-subscriptions/). Licences bought on the website are different: see [Refunds and cancellation](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/refunds-and-cancellation/).
