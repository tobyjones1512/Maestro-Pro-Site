# Medley mode: copy, cut and paste bars

Path: Guide > Write and keep > Writing & Editing Music > Medley mode: copy, cut and paste bars
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/editor-medley-mode/
Summary: Drag across bars to choose them, then Copy, Cut, Paste, Duplicate or Delete, or Insert from Score.

Drag across bars to choose them, then **Copy**, **Cut**, **Paste**, **Duplicate** or **Delete**. **Insert from Score** takes bars from another score in your library, which is how you build a medley.
