# Open the guide and support from the app

Path: Guide > Settings and help > Settings & Accessibility > Open the guide and support from the app
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/open-the-guide-and-support/
Summary: Settings has Guide and Support buttons that open this website inside the app.

Settings has two buttons for help: **Guide** opens this guide and **Support** opens the [support page](https://maestro-pro.app/support/). Both open inside the app, so you do not leave it. On a Mac, the Help menu and the Add menu on the home screen still open the short built-in help.
