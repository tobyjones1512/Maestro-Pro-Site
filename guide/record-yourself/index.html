    Record yourself

    Choose Record: the round record button in the toolbar, More ▸ Record Myself, or ⌘R on a Mac. After the count-in the score plays and you play along. Press Stop, or pause, when you have finished.

    While you record, the built-in instrument and the metronome are muted, so the microphone hears only you. The first time, the app asks to use the microphone.

    With a MIDI instrument connected, Record follows its keys instead of the microphone, and nothing is muted: see Follow a MIDI instrument instead of the microphone.
