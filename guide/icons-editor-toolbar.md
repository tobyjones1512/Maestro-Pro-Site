# Editor toolbar icons

Path: Guide > Settings and help > Buttons and icons > Editor toolbar icons
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/icons-editor-toolbar/
Summary: What each icon does: Listen, Help, Undo, Redo and more.

- {{icon:playu2efill}} **Listen**: plays the music from the chosen note or bar. It becomes a Stop button while playing.
- {{icon:questionmarku2ecircle}} **Help**: opens this guide at the editor pages.
- {{icon:arrowu2euturnu2ebackward}} **Undo**: takes back the last change.
- {{icon:arrowu2euturnu2eforward}} **Redo**: puts back a change you undid.
- {{icon:ellipsisu2ecircle}} **Options**: the sound used for listening, beats per bar, editing with the Pencil only, history and more.
- {{icon:clocku2earrowu2ecirclepath}} **History**: saved versions and branches of the score.
- {{icon:xmarku2ecircle}} **Deselect**: clears whatever is chosen. It shows in the middle of the toolbar while something is chosen.
