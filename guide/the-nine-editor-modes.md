# The nine editor modes

Path: Guide > Write and keep > Writing & Editing Music > The nine editor modes
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/the-nine-editor-modes/
Summary: One strip of nine modes at the bottom of the editor: Notes, Rests, Marks, Dynamics, Bars, Form, Medley, Erase and Draw.

The strip at the bottom of the editor has one mode for each kind of job, so the controls for a job are together. Swipe the strip for the rest. The modes are:

- [Notes](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/editor-notes-mode/)
- [Rests](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/editor-rests-mode/)
- [Marks](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/editor-marks-mode/)
- [Dynamics](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/editor-dynamics-mode/)
- [Bars](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/editor-bars-mode/)
- [Form](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/editor-form-mode/)
- [Medley](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/editor-medley-mode/)
- [Erase](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/editor-erase-mode/)
- [Draw](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/editor-draw-mode/)
