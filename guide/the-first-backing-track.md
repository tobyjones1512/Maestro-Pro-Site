# The first track keeps time

Path: Guide > Sound and words > Backing Tracks & Sound > The first track keeps time
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/the-first-backing-track/
Summary: The first backing track is the one the score keeps time with; later tracks follow its speed and key.

The first track is the one the score keeps time with, and Match Key & Tempo and the track editor work on it. The other tracks follow its speed and key, but not its edit lanes, and should be recordings that start together with it.
