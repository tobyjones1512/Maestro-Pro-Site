# Read the tuner display

Path: Guide > Practise > Tuner & Wait for Me > Read the tuner display
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/read-the-tuner-display/
Summary: It shows the note name, how many cents sharp or flat you are, and the frequency, with a needle and an eight-second trace.

Play a note and the display shows its name, how many cents sharp or flat it is (a cent is a hundredth of a semitone), and the frequency. The needle sits in the green zone when you are in tune, and the trace below shows the last eight seconds.
