# Using Play Together in lessons and rehearsals

Path: Guide > Together > Play Together > Using Play Together in lessons and rehearsals
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/play-together-in-lessons/
Summary: Play, pause, seek and tempo changes on any device are mirrored on all of them.

Play, pause, seek and tempo changes on any device are mirrored on all of them, so a teacher and students, or a whole ensemble, stay in step. Each player keeps their own part, key and instrument.
