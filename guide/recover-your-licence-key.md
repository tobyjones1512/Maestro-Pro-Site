# Recover a lost licence key

Path: Guide > Plans and licences > Licences and keys > Recover a lost licence key
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/recover-your-licence-key/
Summary: Enter the email address you paid with and the key is emailed to you.

Open the [key page](https://maestro-pro.app/key.html), enter the email address you paid with, and press **Email me my key**. If we find your payment, we email you your licence key along with the TestFlight link to install the app.

The key is only ever sent to your inbox, never shown on the page. Use the email address you paid with. If nothing arrives, check your spam folder.
