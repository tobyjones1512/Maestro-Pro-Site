# Start from any bar

Path: Guide > Start here > Playing a Score > Start from any bar
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/jump-to-a-bar/
Summary: Tap the music to jump to that point, or drag the progress bar.

Tap anywhere on the music to start from there. You can also drag the progress bar, or skip back and forward by bar.
