# Cancel a monthly licence

Path: Guide > Plans and licences > Refunds and cancellation > Cancel a monthly licence
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/cancel-a-monthly-licence/
Summary: Monthly licences are on a 12-month contract, so cancelling early costs one month plus half of the remaining term.

Monthly licences are on a 12-month contract. After the 14 days, ending it early costs one month plus half of the remaining term. The cancellation page shows the exact amount before you confirm, and collects it from the bank account you already paid from. Your remaining monthly payments are then cancelled and your key keeps working until the date in the confirmation email.
