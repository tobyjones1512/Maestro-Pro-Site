# Bars mode: repeats, adding and deleting bars

Path: Guide > Write and keep > Writing & Editing Music > Bars mode: repeats, adding and deleting bars
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/editor-bars-mode/
Summary: Repeat signs, adding a bar before or after, deleting one or several bars, and Expand Repeats to write a piece out as it is played.

Tap a bar to choose it. **Start** and **End** put repeat signs on it (End cycles through two, three and four times). **Add Bar Before** and **Add Bar After** put an empty bar, the same length and resting in every staff, next to it. Before the first bar, the new bar takes over the key, time signature, clefs and tempo, so the piece still opens the same way. **Delete Bar** takes the chosen bar out; the last bar of a piece always stays.

To delete several bars at once, touch a bar and drag across the others to choose a run of bars. The button then reads **Delete 3 Bars** (or however many you chose). Tap a single bar again to go back to choosing one.

**Expand Repeats** writes the piece out as it is played.

You can also touch and hold a bar for a menu with all of this. See [The touch and hold menu](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/editor-context-menu/).
