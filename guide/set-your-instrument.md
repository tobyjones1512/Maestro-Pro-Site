# Set your instrument

Path: Guide > Start here > Playing a Score > Set your instrument
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/set-your-instrument/
Summary: Setting your instrument to B flat or E flat in the Inspector shows the written note in the tuner.

Set your instrument in the Inspector. If it is set to B♭ or E♭, the tuner shows the written note too. See [Tune a B♭ or E♭ instrument](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/tune-a-b-flat-or-e-flat-instrument/).
