# Use the metronome

Path: Guide > Start here > Playing a Score > Use the metronome
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/use-the-metronome/
Summary: The metronome clicks on every beat and can be turned on and off, with K on a Mac.

The **Metronome** clicks on every beat. On a Mac, **K** turns it on or off. While you record, the metronome is muted so the microphone hears only you.
