# Add a backing track

Path: Guide > Sound and words > Backing Tracks & Sound > Add a backing track
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/add-a-backing-track/
Summary: Add the first backing track with Add Backing Track, and it plays in time with the score.

A backing track is a recording that plays with the score. Add the first with **Add Backing Track**. You can play along with your own audio files even on the Free plan; backing tracks and Apple Music with all their tools are part of Performer and Professional.
