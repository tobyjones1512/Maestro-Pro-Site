# Open the sample score

Path: Guide > Start here > Getting Started > Open the sample score
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/open-the-sample-score/
Summary: Try the app straight away with the built-in sample, no files needed.

Nothing to play yet? Choose **Open Sample** on the home screen and the app opens a sample score for you to try.

You can also use **Search Scores** to download classical pieces and jazz tunes, or to search the web. Searching needs an internet connection.
