# The Performer plan

Path: Guide > Plans and licences > Plans and pricing > The Performer plan
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/performer-plan/
Summary: Everything except the score editor, with a library of any size.

Performer includes everything except the score editor. You get a library of any size, and notes, bars and a moving line that follow the music.

It also includes Search Scores and lyrics online, key changes and the equaliser, backing tracks and Apple Music, Play Together, the tuner, Wait for Me, recording and review, annotation, and export and print.

Performer is a subscription you start inside the app, managed in your Apple Account settings. It is not sold on this website.
