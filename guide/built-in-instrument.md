# The built-in instrument

Path: Guide > Sound and words > Backing Tracks & Sound > The built-in instrument
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/built-in-instrument/
Summary: The built-in instrument can play the notes on the page, as can a metronome.

The built-in instrument can play the notes on the page too. You can rehearse against it, a backing track, a song from Apple Music, or just a metronome. On the Free plan the synth instruments play the score but nothing follows along on the page.
