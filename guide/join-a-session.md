# Join a session

Path: Guide > Together > Play Together > Join a session
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/join-a-session/
Summary: Choose Join a Session and pick the host's device.

Everyone else chooses **Join a Session** and picks the host's device. Each player keeps their own part, key and instrument; devices line up bar by bar.
