# Tab tunings, bass, ukulele and capo

Path: Guide > Write and keep > Writing & Editing Music > Tab tunings, bass, ukulele and capo
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/tab-tunings-and-capo/
Summary: Give a tab staff another tuning (Drop D, DADGAD, Open G, bass, ukulele) or a capo from the staff's menu.

A tab staff starts as a guitar in standard tuning. To change it:

1. Open the [score editor](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/open-the-score-editor/) and choose **Bars** mode.
2. Open the bar's menu on the tab staff, then the staff's menu (or open **Staves**).
3. Choose **Tuning** and pick one: Guitar, Drop D, DADGAD, Open G, Half Step Down, Bass, Bass 5 String or Ukulele.
4. Choose **Capo** and a fret to put a capo on. Fret numbers are then counted from the capo.

The staff takes as many lines as the instrument has strings, and every note finds its new fret. The notes still sound the same. A note the instrument cannot reach shows as a cross (×).

Tuning and capo are saved with the score and written into exported MusicXML, so other apps show the same tab.
