# Write two voices on one staff

Path: Guide > Write and keep > Writing & Editing Music > Write two voices on one staff
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/two-voices-on-a-staff/
Summary: Add Second Voice gives a bar a second row of rests to write a second rhythm on the same staff.

A staff can carry two rhythms at once, for example a tune over a held note.

1. In the score editor choose **Bars** mode. To do several bars at once, drag across them first.
2. Open the bar's menu, then the staff's menu, and choose **Add Second Voice**.
3. The bar gets a second row of rests, lower on the staff. In **Notes** mode write on those rests as usual.

The upper voice has its stems up and the lower voice has them down. **Remove Second Voice** takes the lower voice out of the bar, with its notes. A staff can have two voices.
