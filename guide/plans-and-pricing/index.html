Plans and pricing

Free, Performer and Professional: what each plan includes, and how to buy.

Maestro Pro has three plans: Free, Performer and Professional. The paid plans are subscriptions, managed in your Apple Account settings, and their prices are shown in the app in your own currency.

Buying on this website is for Professional only, not Performer. It is £10 per month on a 12-month contract or £89 per year, or the equivalent in your local currency. That is cheaper than buying the Performer plan in the app, and it includes the score editor.

More on this topic

  • The Free planWhat you get for nothing: up to three scores, viewing, lyrics files, your own audio, GitHub and scales.
  • The Performer planEverything except the score editor, with a library of any size.
  • The Professional planEverything, including the score editor, medleys and version history with GitHub sync.
