# My MIDI instrument is not detected

Path: Guide > Settings and help > Troubleshooting > My MIDI instrument is not detected
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/midi-instrument-not-detected/
Summary: Check the cable and adapter, that the instrument is on, and Settings, Listening, which shows the connected MIDI instrument.

1. Open **Settings ▸ Listening**. **MIDI Instrument** shows the name of what is connected, or **None connected**.
2. Check the instrument is switched on and the USB cable carries data (some cables only charge). An iPad with a USB-C port takes the cable directly; one with a Lightning port needs Apple's camera adapter.
3. Unplug the instrument and plug it in again. The app picks it up without restarting.
4. A Bluetooth MIDI instrument has to be paired with the iPad or Mac first, in the device's own Bluetooth MIDI settings or the instrument maker's app.
5. In Settings ▸ Listening ▸ Listen With, choose **Automatic** or **MIDI Instrument**. With **Microphone** chosen, Wait for Me and Record ignore MIDI. The score editor always takes notes from a connected MIDI instrument.
6. The instrument must send ordinary MIDI notes. A keyboard that only makes its own sound, with no MIDI or USB port, cannot be followed this way; use the microphone.
