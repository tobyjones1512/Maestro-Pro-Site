# Editing window buttons

Path: Guide > Settings and help > Buttons and icons > Editing window buttons
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/icons-editor-window/
Summary: What each icon does: Close, Minimise, Fit to Contents, Back.

- {{icon:xmark}} **Close**: the red button closes the editing window.
- {{icon:minus}} **Minimise**: the yellow button shrinks the window to its title bar. Double-tap the title bar to bring it back.
- {{icon:arrowu2eupu2eleftu2eandu2earrowu2edownu2eright}} **Fit to Contents**: the green button sizes the window to show everything in it.
- {{icon:chevronu2eleft}} **Back**: goes back from a submenu to the list.
