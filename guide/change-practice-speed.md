# Slow down or speed up without changing pitch

Path: Guide > Start here > Playing a Score > Slow down or speed up without changing pitch
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/change-practice-speed/
Summary: Practice Speed runs from 10 to 400 percent and keeps the pitch the same.

**Practice Speed** slows a piece down or speeds it up without changing the pitch. Type any percentage from 10 to 400 in the box, or use the slider (50 to 150). On a Mac or with a keyboard, [ slows it and ] speeds it up.
