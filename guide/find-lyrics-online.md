# Find lyrics and singers online

Path: Guide > Sound and words > Lyrics & Karaoke > Find lyrics and singers online
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/find-lyrics-online/
Summary: Lyrics and singers can be found online; this needs the internet and sends the song title and artist to lyric services.

The app can find lyrics and singers online when you ask it to. Searching sends your search words (and, to match singers, the song title and artist) to LRCLIB (lyrics) and Genius (singers). Nothing is sent unless you ask. See [When the app uses the internet](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/when-the-app-uses-the-internet/).
