# Manage your licence in the app

Path: Guide > Plans and licences > Licences and keys > Manage your licence in the app
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/manage-your-licence-in-the-app/
Summary: With a licence key, Settings shows Manage Licence: remove a device or request a cancellation.

When your plan comes from a licence key, **Settings** shows **Manage Licence** in place of Manage Subscription (which is for plans bought with the App Store).

Tap **Manage Licence** and choose:

- **Remove A Device** opens the [devices page](https://maestro-pro.app/key/devices/), where you enter your key to see the devices using it and remove one. See [Remove a device from your licence](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/remove-a-device/).
- **Request Cancellation** opens the [cancellation page](https://maestro-pro.app/cancel/), which checks your key and shows what will happen before anything is cancelled or charged. See [How to request a refund or cancel](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/how-to-request-a-refund/).

Both pages open inside the app.
