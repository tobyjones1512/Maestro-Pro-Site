# Import from another app's share sheet

Path: Guide > Start here > Your Library > Import from another app's share sheet
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/import-from-another-app/
Summary: Choose Maestro Pro in the share sheet of Files, Mail, Safari, Messages and other apps.

Choose **Maestro Pro** from the share sheet of another app (Files, Mail, Safari, Messages and others) to import what you are looking at. This works for a score attached to an email or found on a website.
