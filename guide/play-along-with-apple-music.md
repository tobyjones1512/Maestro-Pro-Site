# Play along with Apple Music

Path: Guide > Sound and words > Backing Tracks & Sound > Play along with Apple Music
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/play-along-with-apple-music/
Summary: Play a song from your Apple Music subscription alongside a score and see its lyrics.

With an Apple Music subscription you can play a song alongside a score and see its lyrics. It uses Apple's own service under your account. Apple Music is part of the Performer and Professional plans.
