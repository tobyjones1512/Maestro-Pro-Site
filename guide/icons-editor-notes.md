# Editing window icons for notes

Path: Guide > Settings and help > Buttons and icons > Editing window icons for notes
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/icons-editor-notes/
Summary: What each icon does: Sharp, Flat, Natural, Tie to the Next Note and more.

- {{icon:mu3aE262}} **Sharp**: puts a sharp on the chosen notes.
- {{icon:mu3aE260}} **Flat**: puts a flat on the chosen notes.
- {{icon:mu3aE261}} **Natural**: puts a natural on the chosen notes.
- {{icon:tu3aTie}} **Tie to the Next Note**: joins a note to the next one of the same pitch.
- {{icon:tu3au2022}} **Dotted**: makes the note half as long again.
- {{icon:docu2eonu2edoc}} **Copy Notes**: copies the chosen notes.
- {{icon:scissors}} **Cut Notes**: copies the chosen notes and removes them.
- {{icon:docu2eonu2eclipboard}} **Paste Notes**: opens a choice. Overwrite writes the copied notes over the ones already there and removes them. Insert puts them into new bars after this one.
- {{icon:plusu2esquareu2eonu2esquare}} **Duplicate Notes**: opens a choice: Inline puts the copy straight after, On Stave Below or To Stave Above puts it on the next staff, and To New Stave makes a new staff for it.
- {{icon:trash}} **Turn into a Rest**: replaces the chosen notes with a rest. For a rest, it takes the rest off the page.
- {{icon:minus}} **Half as Long**: halves the length of the chosen notes.
- {{icon:plus}} **Twice as Long**: doubles the length of the chosen notes.
- {{icon:arrowu2eupu2earrowu2edown}} **Pitch**: three rows of buttons to move the chosen notes up or down by a Half Step, a Full Step or an Octave.
- {{icon:arrowu2eup}} **Up**: raises the notes by the step named in its row.
- {{icon:arrowu2edown}} **Down**: lowers the notes by the step named in its row.
- {{icon:arrowu2eupu2etou2eline}} **Up an Octave**: raises the notes by an octave.
- {{icon:arrowu2edownu2etou2eline}} **Down an Octave**: lowers the notes by an octave.
- {{icon:musicu2enoteu2elist}} **Generate Harmony**: writes a harmony a third, fourth, fifth, sixth or octave above or below on an empty staff, or a new one.
- {{icon:checklistu2echecked}} **Select All Notes**: chooses every note in the piece.
- {{icon:checklist}} **Select All Notes on Staff**: chooses every note on one staff.
- {{icon:rectangleu2esplitu2e3x1}} **Select All Bars**: chooses every bar, so bar actions apply to all of them.
- {{icon:chevronu2erightu2e2}} **Articulation**: staccato, accent, tenuto, marcato, fermata and more.
- {{icon:speakeru2ewaveu2e2}} **Dynamic**: sets a dynamic at the note, from ppp to fff.
- {{icon:arrowu2ecounterclockwise}} **Follow the Key Signature**: takes off a note's own accidental so the key signature decides.
- {{icon:rectangleu2esplitu2e3x1}} **Bar**: opens the settings for the note's bar in a second window.
