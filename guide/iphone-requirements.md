# iPhone requirements

Path: Guide > Your devices and privacy > Devices and compatibility > iPhone requirements
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/iphone-requirements/
Summary: iOS 17 or later, which covers iPhone XS and later, including iPhone XR and iPhone SE (2nd generation) and later.

Maestro Pro needs iOS 17 or later. That covers iPhone XS and later, including iPhone XR and iPhone SE (2nd generation) and later. The layout adapts to the screen: one column on a phone held upright, and more room when it is turned on its side.
