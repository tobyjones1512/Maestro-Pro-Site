# Enter your licence key in the app

Path: Guide > Plans and licences > Licences and keys > Enter your licence key in the app
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/enter-your-licence-key/
Summary: On the paywall, tap Already have a licence key? and type your key.

1. Open Maestro Pro.
2. On the paywall, tap **Already have a licence key?**
3. Enter your licence key.

The key works on up to 3 devices. If you are told the limit has been reached, [remove a device](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/remove-a-device/) you no longer use and enter the key again.

If you do not have your key to hand, [recover your licence key](https://maestro-pro.app/key.html) first.
