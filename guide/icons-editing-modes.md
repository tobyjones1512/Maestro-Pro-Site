# Editing mode icons

Path: Guide > Settings and help > Buttons and icons > Editing mode icons
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/icons-editing-modes/
Summary: What each icon does: Notes, Rests, Marks, Dynamics and more.

- {{icon:pencilu2etip}} **Notes**: put in and move notes, and set how long they last.
- {{icon:pause}} **Rests**: add, change and remove rests.
- {{icon:textformatu2eabc}} **Marks**: accidentals, ties and articulations.
- {{icon:speakeru2ewaveu2e2}} **Dynamics**: loud and soft, crescendo and diminuendo.
- {{icon:repeat}} **Bars**: repeat signs, and adding or removing bars.
- {{icon:arrowu2euturnu2eright}} **Form**: first and second time bars, jumps and tempo.
- {{icon:docu2eonu2edoc}} **Medley**: copy, cut, paste and insert bars, even from other scores.
- {{icon:eraser}} **Erase**: rub out notes and rests.
- {{icon:scribbleu2evariable}} **Draw**: write over the music with Apple Pencil.
