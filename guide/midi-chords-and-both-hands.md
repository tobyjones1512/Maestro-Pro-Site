# Chords and both hands in Wait for Me by MIDI

Path: Guide > Practise > Tuner & Wait for Me > Chords and both hands in Wait for Me by MIDI
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/midi-chords-and-both-hands/
Summary: With a MIDI instrument, Wait for Me wants every note of a chord, and can ask for both staves of a piano score at once.

When Wait for Me [follows a MIDI instrument](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/follow-a-midi-instrument/), a chord counts only when every note of it is held down. Part of the chord shows nothing yet; a key that is not in the chord shows as a wrong note. With **Any Octave** on, each note can be played in any octave.

To practise a piano score with both hands:

1. Start Wait for Me with the MIDI instrument connected.
2. Tap the options button (the sliders) in the Wait for Me panel.
3. Under **Part to Follow** choose **All Staves (Both Hands)**.

The score then waits for everything that sounds together on all the staves. Choose a single staff again to practise one hand.

With the microphone, a chord still counts when one of its notes is heard, and one staff is followed at a time.
