# Find free scores with Search Scores

Path: Guide > Start here > Getting Started > Find free scores with Search Scores
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/find-free-scores/
Summary: Search Scores downloads classical pieces and jazz tunes, and searches the web for free scores.

Choose **Search Scores** on the home screen to download classical pieces and jazz tunes, or to search the web for free scores. Downloading a free score fetches it from the site that hosts it.

Search Scores needs the internet, and it is part of the Performer and Professional plans. The Free plan does not include anything that needs the internet.
