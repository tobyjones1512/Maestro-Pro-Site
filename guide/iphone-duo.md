# iPhone Duo

Path: Guide > Your devices and privacy > Devices and compatibility > iPhone Duo
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/iphone-duo/
Summary: On iPhone Duo the music and controls use both screens, and the playback bar folds away for lyrics.

iPhone Duo is supported. The music and the controls use the space across both screens, and the playback bar folds away to give lyrics and karaoke the full screen.
