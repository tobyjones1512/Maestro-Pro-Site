Install Maestro Pro step by step

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store on your iPhone, iPad or Mac.
  2. Open the Maestro Pro invitation and tap Accept, then Install.
  3. Open Maestro Pro. Tap "Already have a licence key?" on the paywall and enter your key.

The install link comes in the email that carries your licence key.
