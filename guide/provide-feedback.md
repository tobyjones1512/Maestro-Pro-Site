# Provide feedback from the app

Path: Guide > Settings and help > Settings & Accessibility > Provide feedback from the app
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/provide-feedback/
Summary: Provide Feedback in Settings posts your title and description to the public feedback board.

**Provide Feedback** in Settings posts what you type, the title and description, to the public feedback board, tagged for Maestro Pro. You can also use the [feedback page](https://maestro-pro.app/feedback/) on the website. Anything you send can be read by anyone, so leave out personal details.
