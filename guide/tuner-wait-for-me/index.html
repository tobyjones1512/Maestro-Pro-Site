    Tuner & Wait for Me

    Maestro Pro can listen through the microphone: to tune your instrument, to train a steady note, and to wait for you to play a score note by note. Sound is analysed on the device and is never recorded or sent anywhere.

    • The tuner

    The tuner

    Open the Tuner from the home screen, the + menu, Settings ▸ Listening, or the tuning fork in a score's toolbar. Play a note: the display shows its name, how many cents sharp or flat it is (a cent is a hundredth of a semitone), and the frequency. The needle sits in the green zone when you are in tune, and the trace below shows the last eight seconds.

    If your instrument is set to B♭ or E♭ in the Inspector, the written note is shown too. Play Reference Note sounds the note you are aiming for. A = 440 Hz can be changed (415 to 466) for baroque or orchestral tuning.

    More on this topic

    • Open the tunerThe Tuner is on the home screen, the + menu, Settings under Listening, and the tuning fork in a score's toolbar.
    • Read the tuner displayIt shows the note name, how many cents sharp or flat you are, and the frequency, with a needle and an eight-second trace.
    • Play a reference notePlay Reference Note sounds the note you are aiming for.
    • Change the tuning pitch (A = 440 Hz)A = 440 Hz can be changed between 415 and 466 for baroque or orchestral tuning.
    • Tune a B flat or E flat instrumentWith your instrument set to B flat or E flat in the Inspector, the tuner shows the written note too.
    • What is Wait for Me?Wait for Me holds the score until the right note is played in tune.
    • Follow a MIDI instrument instead of the microphoneWith a MIDI instrument connected, Wait for Me and Record follow the keys you press instead of listening through the microphone.
    • The steady-note trainerA trainer that listens as you hold a note steady, alongside the tuner and Wait for Me.
