# The Inspector

Path: Guide > Start here > Playing a Score > The Inspector
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/use-the-inspector/
Summary: The Inspector holds tempo, practice speed, loop, key, instrument and backing tracks.

The toolbar and the Inspector change how a score looks and sounds. The Inspector is where you find tempo, practice speed, loop, key, instrument and backing tracks, and where you can add another backing track.
