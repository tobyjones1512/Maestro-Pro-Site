Refunds and cancellation

The 14-day money-back guarantee, and how to cancel a licence bought on the website.

Licences bought on the website have a 14-day money-back guarantee. Refunds and cancellations cannot be done by email: they are requested on the cancellation page, which checks your licence key and shows exactly what will happen before anything is cancelled or charged.

More on this topic

  • The 14-day money-back guaranteeAsk for a full refund within 14 days of your first payment, once the payment has cleared.
  • How to request a refund or cancelUse the cancellation page with your licence key and follow the steps.
  • When can I get a refund?Refunds are made from day 8 to day 14 after the first payment, once it has cleared.
  • Cancelling after the 14 daysNo refund, but you can cancel going forward, and how that works differs for yearly and monthly plans.
  • Cancel a yearly licenceYour yearly subscription is cancelled and the key works until the end of the year you paid for.
  • Cancel a monthly licenceMonthly licences are on a 12-month contract, so cancelling early costs one month plus half of the remaining term.
