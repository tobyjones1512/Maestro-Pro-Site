# Editing window icons for notes (1 of 3)

Path: Guide > Settings and help > Buttons and icons > Editing window icons for notes (1 of 3)
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/icons-editor-notes-1/
Summary: What each icon does: Sharp, Flat, Natural, Tie to the Next Note and more.

- {{icon:mu3aE262}} **Sharp**: puts a sharp on the chosen notes.
- {{icon:mu3aE260}} **Flat**: puts a flat on the chosen notes.
- {{icon:mu3aE261}} **Natural**: puts a natural on the chosen notes.
- {{icon:tu3aTie}} **Tie to the Next Note**: joins a note to the next one of the same pitch.
- {{icon:tu3au2022}} **Dotted**: makes the note half as long again.
- {{icon:docu2eonu2edoc}} **Copy Notes**: copies the chosen notes.
- {{icon:scissors}} **Cut Notes**: copies the chosen notes and removes them.
- {{icon:docu2eonu2eclipboard}} **Paste Notes**: opens a choice. Overwrite writes the copied notes over the ones already there and removes them. Insert puts them into new bars after this one.
- {{icon:plusu2esquareu2eonu2esquare}} **Duplicate Notes**: opens a choice: Inline puts the copy straight after, On Stave Below or To Stave Above puts it on the next staff, and To New Stave makes a new staff for it.
- {{icon:trash}} **Turn into a Rest**: replaces the chosen notes with a rest. For a rest, it takes the rest off the page.
- {{icon:minus}} **Half as Long**: halves the length of the chosen notes.
- {{icon:plus}} **Twice as Long**: doubles the length of the chosen notes.
