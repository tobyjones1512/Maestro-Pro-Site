# Import a score

Path: Guide > Start here > Your Library > Import a score
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/import-a-score/
Summary: Tap Import Score, choose your files, and they appear in the library.

Tap **Import Score** on the home screen (or the + button, then Import Score) and choose a file. It appears in your library. Tap it to open it.

You can choose several files at once. Other ways in are dragging files onto the window, the share sheet of another app, and importing a whole folder.
