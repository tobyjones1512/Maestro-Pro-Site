# Editing window icons for bars (1 of 2)

Path: Guide > Settings and help > Buttons and icons > Editing window icons for bars (1 of 2)
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/icons-editor-bars-1/
Summary: What each icon does: Copy Bar, Cut Bar, Paste Bars, Duplicate and more.

- {{icon:docu2eonu2edoc}} **Copy Bar**: copies the chosen bars.
- {{icon:scissors}} **Cut Bar**: copies the chosen bars and removes them.
- {{icon:docu2eonu2eclipboard}} **Paste Bars**: opens a choice. Overwrite replaces the chosen bars with the copied ones. Insert puts the copied bars in after the chosen ones.
- {{icon:plusu2esquareu2eonu2esquare}} **Duplicate**: opens a choice of Inline, On Stave Below, To Stave Above or To New Stave.
- {{icon:trash}} **Delete Bar**: removes the chosen bars. The last bar of a piece always stays.
- {{icon:tu3au2016u3a}} **Repeat start**: puts a repeat sign at the start of the bar.
- {{icon:tu3au3au2016}} **Repeat end**: puts a repeat sign at the end of the bar, and cycles through the number of times.
- {{icon:plus}} **Add a bar after**: adds an empty bar after this one.
- {{icon:plusu2erectangle}} **Add Bar After**: adds an empty bar after the chosen bar.
- {{icon:plusu2erectangleu2eonu2erectangle}} **Add Bar Before**: adds an empty bar before the chosen bar.
- {{icon:checklist}} **Select Content**: chooses all the notes in the chosen bars.
- {{icon:chevronu2eleftu2eforwardslashu2echevronu2eright}} **Crescendo and Diminuendo**: puts a hairpin over the bar.
