Buy for a whole organisation

You can buy one licence that covers a whole school, college, music service or tuition business, straight from us and outside the App Store. It is £499 a year for unlimited students and teachers, with every Maestro Pro feature, including the score editor, medleys and version history with GitHub sync.

There is no per-seat counting and no Apple School Manager or Apple Business Manager set-up. An organisation licence has no device limit, unlike a single licence, which works on up to 3 devices (see Use your licence on up to 3 devices). You pay through our secure payment page, then recover your licence key with the email address you paid with. The details are on the education page.
