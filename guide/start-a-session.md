# Start a Play Together session

Path: Guide > Together > Play Together > Start a Play Together session
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/start-a-session/
Summary: Open your parts, choose Play Together, then Start a Session, and allow each player in.

Open your parts, then one person chooses **Play Together**, then **Start a Session**. The host is asked to allow each player in. Up to eight devices can join one session. Play Together is part of the Performer and Professional plans and works in Advanced mode.
