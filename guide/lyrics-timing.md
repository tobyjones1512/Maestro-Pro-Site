# Lyrics in seconds or in bars

Path: Guide > Sound and words > Lyrics & Karaoke > Lyrics in seconds or in bars
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/lyrics-timing/
Summary: When a lyrics file has no timing, you can choose whether lyrics are timed in seconds or in bars.

Lyrics in seconds or in bars can be chosen when the file has no timing, such as a plain .txt file.
