# A backing track starts late or early

Path: Guide > Settings and help > Troubleshooting > A backing track starts late or early
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/backing-track-starts-late-or-early/
Summary: Drag the track marker, or use Delay Start, then use Match Key and Tempo to Track to listen again.

Drag the track marker, or use **Delay Start**. Use **Match Key & Tempo to Track** to listen again. For a track with an introduction, set [Lead-in Bars](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/count-in-and-lead-in-bars/).
