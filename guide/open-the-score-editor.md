# Open the score editor

Path: Guide > Write and keep > Writing & Editing Music > Open the score editor
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/open-the-score-editor/
Summary: Edit Score opens the editor, which works on MusicXML scores and on a copy of what the app read from a PDF.

The score editor changes the notes in a MusicXML score. Open it with **Edit Score**, the music-note-list button. In Simple mode the button appears for scores you created. The editor is part of the Professional plan.
