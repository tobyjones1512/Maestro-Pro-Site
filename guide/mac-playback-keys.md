# Playback keys on a Mac

Path: Guide > Start here > Playing a Score > Playback keys on a Mac
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/mac-playback-keys/
Summary: Space plays and pauses, arrows skip bars, Return goes to the start and K toggles the metronome.

On a Mac, **Space** plays and pauses, the **arrow keys** skip bars, **Return** goes to the start and **K** turns the metronome on or off. On a Mac or with a keyboard, **[** slows the practice speed and **]** speeds it up. You can change any shortcut: see [Keyboard shortcuts](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/keyboard-shortcuts/).
