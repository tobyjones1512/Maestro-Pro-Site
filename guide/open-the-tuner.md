# Open the tuner

Path: Guide > Practise > Tuner & Wait for Me > Open the tuner
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/open-the-tuner/
Summary: The Tuner is on the home screen, the + menu, Settings under Listening, and the tuning fork in a score's toolbar.

Open the **Tuner** from the home screen, the + menu, Settings ▸ Listening, or the tuning fork in a score's toolbar. The tuner listens through the microphone, so allow microphone access when asked. Sound is analysed on the device and is never recorded or sent anywhere. The tuner is part of the Performer and Professional plans.
