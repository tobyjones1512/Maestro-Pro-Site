# Annotate on a Mac

Path: Guide > Practise > Annotating > Annotate on a Mac
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/annotate-on-a-mac/
Summary: On a Mac you draw with the trackpad or mouse instead of Apple Pencil.

Annotating on iPad uses Apple Pencil, so your finger still scrolls and taps. On a Mac you draw with the trackpad or mouse.
