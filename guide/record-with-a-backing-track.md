# Record when the score has a backing track

Path: Guide > Practise > Record & Review > Record when the score has a backing track
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/record-with-a-backing-track/
Summary: The app asks first, because the microphone would hear the backing: Mute It and Record, or Record Anyway.

If the score has a backing track, the app asks before recording, because the microphone would hear it. Choose **Mute It and Record** (the track comes back afterwards), or **Record Anyway** if you use headphones. The recording bar has a **Mute It** button too.
