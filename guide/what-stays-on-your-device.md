# What stays on your device

Path: Guide > Your devices and privacy > Privacy and permissions > What stays on your device
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/what-stays-on-your-device/
Summary: Your library is stored in the app's folder on your device, or in your own iCloud if you turn it on.

Your library (scores, backing tracks, lyrics, drawings, recordings and version history) is stored in the app's folder on your device. If you turn on iCloud Drive, it is kept in the app's iCloud folder in your own iCloud account. That storage belongs to you and Apple; the developer cannot see it.
