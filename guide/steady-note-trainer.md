# The steady-note trainer

Path: Guide > Practise > Tuner & Wait for Me > The steady-note trainer
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/steady-note-trainer/
Summary: A trainer that listens as you hold a note steady, alongside the tuner and Wait for Me.

The tuner and Wait for Me are joined by a steady-note trainer, which uses the microphone to help you hold a note steady. It is part of the listening tools in the Performer and Professional plans.
