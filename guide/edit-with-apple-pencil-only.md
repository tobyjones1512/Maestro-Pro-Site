# Stop a resting hand changing notes

Path: Guide > Write and keep > Writing & Editing Music > Stop a resting hand changing notes
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/edit-with-apple-pencil-only/
Summary: The setting Edit with Apple Pencil Only stops a resting hand changing notes.

Editing works with a finger or a Pencil. A setting, **Edit with Apple Pencil Only**, stops a resting hand changing notes.
