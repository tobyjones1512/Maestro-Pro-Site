# Education and schools

Path: Guide > Plans and licences > Education and schools
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/education-and-schools/
Summary: How schools, colleges and tutors can get Maestro Pro, including volume licensing outside the App Store.

Maestro Pro is for music schools, music services, colleges and private tutors as well as individuals. The [education page](https://maestro-pro.app/education/) describes how it helps students practise and tutors teach.

There are two ways to buy for a group: Apple's volume purchasing for the free version, and a single licence for a whole organisation bought direct from us.

## More on this topic
- [Apple volume purchasing](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/apple-volume-purchasing/): Schools can roll the free version out through Apple School Manager or Apple Business Manager.
- [Buy for a whole organisation](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/buy-for-your-organisation/): One licence for unlimited students and teachers, £499 a year, bought direct and outside the App Store.
- [The student licence](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/student-licence/): Students can buy Professional for £48 a year or £6 a month on a 12-month contract.
