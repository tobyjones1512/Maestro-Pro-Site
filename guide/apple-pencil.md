# Apple Pencil

Path: Guide > Your devices and privacy > Devices and compatibility > Apple Pencil
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/apple-pencil/
Summary: Optional: any Apple Pencil that works with your iPad will do, and you do not need one to read, play, transpose or edit.

Apple Pencil is optional. You do not need a Pencil to read, play, transpose or edit. Any Apple Pencil that works with your iPad will do: Apple Pencil (1st generation), (2nd generation), (USB-C) or Pro. Which one your iPad takes is listed on Apple's Pencil compatibility page. Annotating on iPad uses Apple Pencil, so your finger still scrolls and taps.
