# Record what you play into the score, in time

Path: Guide > Write and keep > Writing & Editing Music > Record what you play into the score, in time
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/record-midi-into-the-editor/
Summary: Tap Record in the score editor and play a MIDI instrument to the metronome; the notes are written with their rhythm.

Step entry writes one note at a time at the length you choose. Recording writes the rhythm you actually play.

1. Connect a MIDI instrument (or show the [on-screen piano](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/editor-piano-keyboard/)). The **Record** button, a ring with a dot, appears at the top of the score editor.
2. Hold **Record** and choose what notes snap to: **Quarter Notes**, **Eighth Notes** or **Sixteenth Notes**. Pick the shortest note in what you are about to play.
3. Select the bar to start from and tap **Record**. You hear one bar of count in, then the metronome keeps time.
4. Play. Tap **Stop** when you have finished (the end of the piece also stops it).

Your notes are written from that bar on the selected staff. Notes played together become a chord, and a note held over a barline is tied. The bars you played through are replaced; Undo brings them back in one step.

Things to know:

- It writes one voice, so a note ends when the next one begins.
- It does not write triplets.
- If every note lands a little late or early, change **MIDI Recording Timing** in Settings, Listening.
- The tempo is the score's tempo. Set a slower tempo first if you want more time.
