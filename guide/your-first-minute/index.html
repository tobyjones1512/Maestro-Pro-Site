Your first minute

  1. Tap Import Score on the home screen (or the + button, then Import Score) and choose a PDF, MusicXML file or any other supported file.
  2. The score appears in your library on the left. Tap it to open it.
  3. Press the big Play button at the bottom. The music plays and follows along.

Tap anywhere on the music to start from there, or drag the progress bar.
