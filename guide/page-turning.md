# Automatic page turning

Path: Guide > Start here > Playing a Score > Automatic page turning
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/page-turning/
Summary: The page scrolls or curls over like a real book exactly when you need it.

When you press Play, the music follows itself. The page scrolls or curls over like a real book exactly when you need it, so you do not need to turn pages by hand.
