# Write chord symbols and show guitar chord diagrams

Path: Guide > Write and keep > Writing & Editing Music > Write chord symbols and show guitar chord diagrams
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/chord-symbols-and-diagrams/
Summary: In Marks mode, tap Chord to write a chord over a note; Chords on the Page shows names or names with guitar diagrams.

**Write a chord**

1. In the score editor choose **Marks** mode and tap the note or rest the chord belongs over.
2. Tap **Chord**, choose the root (C, D, E and so on), then the kind: major, m, 7, maj7, m7, sus4, sus2, 6, m6, 9, maj9, m9, dim, dim7, m7♭5, aug or 5.
3. For a slash chord such as D/F♯, tap **Chord** again and choose **Bass Note**.

**Remove Chord** in the same menu takes it away. The chords you write are also what the chord view shows.

**Show them on the page**

Tap **Chord** and, under **Chords on the Page**, choose **Hidden**, **Chord Names** or **Names and Guitar Diagrams**. A diagram shows six strings and four frets with a dot for each finger, × over a string that is not played and ○ over an open string. A score that came with chord symbols can show them the same way.

Diagrams are for a guitar in standard tuning, one common fingering for each chord.
