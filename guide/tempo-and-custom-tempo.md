# Tempo and Custom Tempo

Path: Guide > Start here > Playing a Score > Tempo and Custom Tempo
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/tempo-and-custom-tempo/
Summary: The tempo is the score's own unless you set one; Custom Tempo goes back to the score's.

The tempo is the score's own, from its printed metronome mark, unless you set one. Typing a BPM, tapping it or matching a backing track makes it your own, and the **Custom Tempo** switch under the tempo goes back to the score's.
