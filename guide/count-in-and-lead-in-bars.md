# Count In and Lead-in Bars

Path: Guide > Start here > Playing a Score > Count In and Lead-in Bars
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/count-in-and-lead-in-bars/
Summary: Count In plays a bar of clicks first; Lead-in Bars sets how many bars of introduction come before bar 1.

**Count In** plays a bar of clicks before the music. **Lead-in Bars** sets how many bars of introduction come before bar 1, so a backing track with an intro lines up.
