# Prices and currencies

Path: Guide > Plans and licences > Plans and pricing > Prices and currencies
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/prices-and-currency/
Summary: Website prices are in pounds sterling or the local equivalent, and app prices show in your own currency.

Prices on the website are in pounds sterling, or the equivalent in your local currency. Prices for plans bought in the app are shown in the app, in your own currency.
