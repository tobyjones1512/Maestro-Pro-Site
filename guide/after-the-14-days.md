# Cancelling after the 14 days

Path: Guide > Plans and licences > Refunds and cancellation > Cancelling after the 14 days
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/after-the-14-days/
Summary: No refund, but you can cancel going forward, and how that works differs for yearly and monthly plans.

After the 14 days we cannot refund your payments, but you can cancel so that nothing more is taken. What happens depends on how you pay:

- [Yearly licence](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/cancel-a-yearly-licence/): the subscription is cancelled and your key keeps working to the end of the year you paid for.
- [Monthly licence](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/cancel-a-monthly-licence/): a cancellation fee is taken, then your remaining payments stop.
