# Cancel a yearly licence

Path: Guide > Plans and licences > Refunds and cancellation > Cancel a yearly licence
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/cancel-a-yearly-licence/
Summary: Your yearly subscription is cancelled and the key works until the end of the year you paid for.

After the 14 days, cancelling a yearly licence stops it renewing. Your licence key keeps working until the end of the year you paid for, and the cancellation email gives that date.
