# Show notes with tab underneath

Path: Guide > Write and keep > Writing & Editing Music > Show notes with tab underneath
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/notes-and-tab-together/
Summary: Guitar Tab Underneath prints a staff's music a second time as tab, and both stay in step.

A guitar part is often printed twice: ordinary notes on top and tab below. To get that:

1. In the score editor choose **Bars** mode and open the menu of a staff of ordinary notes (or open **Staves**).
2. Turn on **Guitar Tab Underneath**.

The tab appears below the staves. It is the same music, not a copy: change a note on either staff and both change, and it plays only once. Drag a number on the tab to another string to choose where it is played. Turn the option off to go back to notes only.

The tab is printed under the last staff of the score, which is directly below for a guitar part on its own.
