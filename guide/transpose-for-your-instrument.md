# Transpose for your instrument

Path: Guide > Start here > Playing a Score > Transpose for your instrument
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/transpose-for-your-instrument/
Summary: Transposing typesets the score again in the new key, rather than just labelling it.

Transpose for your clarinet, trumpet or saxophone, and the score is typeset again in the new key rather than just labelled. Key changes are part of the Performer and Professional plans.
