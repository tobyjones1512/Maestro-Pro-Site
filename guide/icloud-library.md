# Keep your library in iCloud

Path: Guide > Start here > Your Library > Keep your library in iCloud
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/icloud-library/
Summary: Your library is stored on the device and is also kept in iCloud when iCloud Drive is on.

Everything you import lives in the library and stays on the device. If you turn on iCloud Drive, it is also kept in the app's iCloud folder in your own iCloud account, so it is available on your other devices. That storage belongs to you and Apple; the developer cannot see it.
