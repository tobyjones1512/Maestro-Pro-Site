# The student licence

Path: Guide > Plans and licences > Education and schools > The student licence
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/student-licence/
Summary: Students can buy Professional for £48 a year or £6 a month on a 12-month contract.

The [students page](https://maestro-pro.app/students/) sells Professional to students at £48 per year, or £6 per month on a 12-month contract. A student licence works on up to 3 devices. After you pay, use the [students key page](https://maestro-pro.app/students/key.html) to have your licence key emailed to you.
