# Remove a device from your licence

Path: Guide > Plans and licences > Licences and keys > Remove a device from your licence
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/remove-a-device/
Summary: Use the devices page on the key page to see where your licence is used and remove a device.

1. Open the [devices page](https://maestro-pro.app/key/devices/).
2. Enter your licence key and choose **Show my devices**.
3. Find the device you no longer use. If you are not sure which it is, open Maestro Pro on it and choose **Refresh**: the device you just used moves to the top with a new last used time.
4. Choose **Remove** next to the device you want to take off.

The place is free straight away, so you can enter your key on another device. A removed device is signed out the next time Maestro Pro checks the licence, which is each time the app is opened. You can use **Remove all devices** to start again.

If you do not have your key, [recover your licence key](https://maestro-pro.app/key.html) first.
