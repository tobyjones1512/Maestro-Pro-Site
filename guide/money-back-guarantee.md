# The 14-day money-back guarantee

Path: Guide > Plans and licences > Refunds and cancellation > The 14-day money-back guarantee
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/money-back-guarantee/
Summary: Ask for a full refund within 14 days of your first payment, once the payment has cleared.

If Maestro Pro is not for you, you can ask for a full refund within 14 days of your first payment. Bank payments take about a week to clear, and a payment can only be refunded once it has. So you can ask from the 8th day on and by the 14th day.

When the refund is made, your licence, subscription and any future payments are cancelled.
