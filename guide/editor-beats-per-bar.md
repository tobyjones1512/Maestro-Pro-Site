# Beats per bar

Path: Guide > Write and keep > Writing & Editing Music > Beats per bar
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/editor-beats-per-bar/
Summary: Options has Beats per Bar: 4, 3 or a custom number, chosen to match the score when the editor opens.

Open **Options** in the editor and choose **Beats per Bar**. The editor ticks 4, 3 or Custom to match the opening time signature of the score. Choose another and every bar in the score takes that many beats, using the same kind of beat as before (so 3 in a 4/4 score gives 3/4). Notes that run past the end of a bar are shortened, notes that start after it are cut, and short bars are filled out with rests. **Custom** asks for a number from 1 to 16. You can undo the change like any other edit.
