# Play the score through a MIDI instrument

Path: Guide > Sound and words > Backing Tracks & Sound > Play the score through a MIDI instrument
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/play-through-a-midi-instrument/
Summary: Settings, Playback, Play Through MIDI Instrument sends the notes to your keyboard or sound module.

Maestro Pro can play a score on a connected MIDI keyboard, digital piano or sound module instead of its built-in sounds.

1. Connect the instrument with USB (or pair a Bluetooth MIDI instrument with the device).
2. Open **Settings**, **Playback**, and turn on **Play Through MIDI Instrument**. **MIDI Output** shows what it found.
3. Play the score. The notes sound on your instrument, in whatever sound it is set to.

The metronome and any backing track still come from the iPad or Mac. All staves are sent on MIDI channel 1. If nothing is connected, the built-in sounds are used. Turn the setting off to go back to the built-in sounds.
