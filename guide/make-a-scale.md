# Make a scale or arpeggio

Path: Guide > Practise > Scales & Arpeggios > Make a scale or arpeggio
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/make-a-scale/
Summary: Choose Scales & Arpeggios on the home screen, pick the kind and a key, and a score is created for you.

Choose **Scales & Arpeggios** on the home screen, pick the kind and a key. A score is created for it and added to the library, ready to play, transpose and slow down.
