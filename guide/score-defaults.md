# Defaults that any score can override

Path: Guide > Settings and help > Settings & Accessibility > Defaults that any score can override
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/score-defaults/
Summary: Zoom, dark sheet music, follow playback and the three highlights have defaults in Settings.

Settings has the choices that apply everywhere. Zoom, dark sheet music, follow playback and the three highlights have defaults there, which any score can override.
