# Hammer-ons, pull-offs, slides, bends and palm mutes

Path: Guide > Write and keep > Writing & Editing Music > Hammer-ons, pull-offs, slides, bends and palm mutes
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/tab-techniques/
Summary: In Marks mode, a note on tab has buttons for Hammer-On, Pull-Off, Slide, Bend and Palm Mute.

1. In the score editor choose **Marks** mode.
2. Tap a note on a tab staff (or on a staff with tab underneath).
3. Tap **Hammer-On**, **Pull-Off**, **Slide**, **Bend** or **Palm Mute**. Tap it again to take it off.

They print over the note as H, P, sl., a bend arrow with "full", and P.M. A hammer-on or pull-off shows its letter; if the notes have a slur, the slur draws the arc. The marks are saved in exported MusicXML. They do not change how the note sounds in playback.
