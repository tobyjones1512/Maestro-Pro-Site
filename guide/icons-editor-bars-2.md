# Editing window icons for bars (2 of 2)

Path: Guide > Settings and help > Buttons and icons > Editing window icons for bars (2 of 2)
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/icons-editor-bars-2/
Summary: What each icon does: Longer, Shorter, First and Second Time Bars, Jumps and Signs and more.

- {{icon:arrowu2erightu2etou2eline}} **Longer**: stretches a hairpin or bracket over more bars.
- {{icon:arrowu2eleftu2etou2eline}} **Shorter**: shortens a hairpin or bracket.
- {{icon:squareu2esplitu2e2x1}} **First and Second Time Bars**: first time, second time and bracket length.
- {{icon:arrowu2euturnu2eright}} **Jumps and Signs**: Segno, Coda, To Coda, D.C., D.S. and Fine.
- {{icon:squareu2eandu2earrowu2edownu2eonu2esquare}} **Insert from Score**: takes bars from another score in your library.
- {{icon:rectangleu2eexpandu2evertical}} **Expand Repeats**: writes the piece out as it is played.
- {{icon:rectangleu2esplitu2e1x2}} **All Staves**: actions that take the bar from every staff at once.
- {{icon:plus}} **Add Treble Staff**: adds a staff with a treble clef.
- {{icon:plus}} **Add Bass Staff**: adds a staff with a bass clef.
- {{icon:trash}} **Delete Staff**: removes one staff and its notes, leaving the others.
