# Website or in-app: which is cheaper?

Path: Guide > Plans and licences > Plans and pricing > Website or in-app: which is cheaper?
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/website-or-in-app/
Summary: Professional bought on the website costs less than Performer bought in the app, and includes the editor.

Buying Professional on this website is cheaper than buying the Performer plan in the app, and it includes the score editor. Performer is not sold on the website.

Plans bought inside the app are subscriptions managed in your Apple Account settings. A licence bought on the website is managed with us: your key arrives by email and cancellation is requested on the [cancellation page](https://maestro-pro.app/cancel/).
