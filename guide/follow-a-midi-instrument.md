# Follow a MIDI instrument instead of the microphone

Path: Guide > Practise > Tuner & Wait for Me > Follow a MIDI instrument instead of the microphone
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/follow-a-midi-instrument/
Summary: With a MIDI instrument connected, Wait for Me and Record follow the keys you press instead of listening through the microphone.

Plug a MIDI keyboard, digital piano or other MIDI instrument into your iPad or Mac with USB. A Bluetooth MIDI instrument works too, once the device has paired with it. **Wait for Me** and **Record** then follow the keys you press instead of listening through the microphone. Nothing needs setting up: the Wait for Me panel says which instrument it is following.

Because the app is told exactly which key is down:

- A noisy room, a quiet instrument or headphones make no difference.
- Tuning never counts against you. Only the right note and holding it long enough matter.
- While you record, the built-in instrument and the metronome stay on, because there is no microphone for them to spoil.
- A recording made this way keeps the notes you played, to review against the score, but no sound.

## Choose what the app listens with

**Settings ▸ Listening ▸ Listen With** has three choices:

- **Automatic** (the default): a MIDI instrument whenever one is connected, otherwise the microphone.
- **Microphone**: always the microphone. Choose this if a MIDI instrument is plugged in but you want to play or sing something else.
- **MIDI Instrument**: always MIDI.

The same Settings page shows which MIDI instrument is connected. The tuner always uses the microphone.
