# Erase mode

Path: Guide > Write and keep > Writing & Editing Music > Erase mode
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/editor-erase-mode/
Summary: Touch or drag over notes and rests to erase them; double-tap Apple Pencil to switch to the eraser.

Touch or drag over notes and rests. A note becomes a rest; a rest comes off the page. Double-tap Apple Pencil to switch to the eraser and back.
