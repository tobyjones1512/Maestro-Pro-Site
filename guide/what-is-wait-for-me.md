# What is Wait for Me?

Path: Guide > Practise > Tuner & Wait for Me > What is Wait for Me?
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/what-is-wait-for-me/
Summary: Wait for Me holds the score until the right note is played in tune.

**Wait for Me** holds the score until you play the right note in tune, so you can work through a piece note by note at your own pace. It listens through the microphone, and the sound is analysed on the device.

Wait for Me is part of the Performer and Professional plans. If it says it cannot hear you, see [The microphone cannot hear me](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/microphone-cannot-hear-me/).
