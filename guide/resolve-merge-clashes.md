# Resolve merge clashes

Path: Guide > Write and keep > Version History & GitHub > Resolve merge clashes
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/resolve-merge-clashes/
Summary: A bar changed on both branches clashes, and you choose to keep yours or take theirs.

A bar changed on both branches clashes, and you choose to keep yours or take theirs. Bars are matched by number, so a bar added or removed early on one branch can make the rest show as clashes.
