# When can I get a refund?

Path: Guide > Plans and licences > Refunds and cancellation > When can I get a refund?
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/refund-timing/
Summary: Refunds are made from day 8 to day 14 after the first payment, once it has cleared.

- **Days 0 to 7:** the payment has not cleared yet, so it cannot be refunded. The cancellation page tells you the date to come back, the 8th day after your payment.
- **Days 8 to 14:** you can be refunded in full.
- **Day 15 onwards:** the guarantee has ended. See [after the 14 days](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/after-the-14-days/).
