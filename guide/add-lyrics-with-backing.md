# Lyrics with Backing

Path: Guide > Start here > Your Library > Lyrics with Backing
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/add-lyrics-with-backing/
Summary: Choose a backing track and the app finds its timed lyrics online for a karaoke-style screen.

**Lyrics with Backing**: choose a backing track and the app finds its timed lyrics online, for a karaoke-style screen. You can also choose a lyrics file along with the backing track. Finding lyrics online needs the internet.
