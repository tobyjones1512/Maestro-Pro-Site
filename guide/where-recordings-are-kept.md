# Where recordings are kept

Path: Guide > Practise > Record & Review > Where recordings are kept
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/where-recordings-are-kept/
Summary: Recordings are kept with the score, copied when you duplicate it, and compressed to well under a megabyte a minute.

Recordings are kept with the score, so they are copied when you duplicate it. Each one is compressed to well under a megabyte a minute. Recordings are saved only in your library on your device, and are never sent to us.
