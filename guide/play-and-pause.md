# Play, pause and skip

Path: Guide > Start here > Playing a Score > Play, pause and skip
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/play-and-pause/
Summary: The bar at the bottom has Play and Pause, skip by bar and a progress bar you can drag.

The bar at the bottom controls playback: Play and Pause, skip back and forward by bar, and a progress bar you can drag. The counter shows the bar, beat and time.
