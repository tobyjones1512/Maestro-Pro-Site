    Education and schools

    How schools, colleges and tutors can get Maestro Pro, including volume licensing outside the App Store.

    Maestro Pro is for music schools, music services, colleges and private tutors as well as individuals. The education page describes how it helps students practise and tutors teach.

    There are two ways to buy for a group: Apple's volume purchasing for the free version, and a single licence for a whole organisation bought direct from us.

    More on this topic

    • Apple volume purchasingSchools can roll the free version out through Apple School Manager or Apple Business Manager.
    • Buy for a whole organisationOne licence for unlimited students and teachers, £499 a year, bought direct and outside the App Store.
    • The student licenceStudents can buy Professional for £48 a year or £6 a month on a 12-month contract.
