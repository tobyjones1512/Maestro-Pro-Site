# Which plan do I need?

Path: Guide > Plans and licences > Plans and pricing > Which plan do I need?
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/compare-plans/
Summary: A quick way to choose between Free, Performer and Professional.

- **Free** is for reading your part: up to three scores, viewing, lyrics files, your own audio, GitHub and scales.
- **Performer** is for practising with the full set of tools, except the editor: highlighting that follows the music, search, backing tracks and Apple Music, Play Together, the tuner, Wait for Me, recording and review, annotation, export and print.
- **Professional** is for anyone who writes or arranges music: everything in Performer, plus the score editor, medleys and version history with GitHub sync.

Teachers and schools can also read [Education and schools](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/education-and-schools/).
