# Export as MusicXML

Path: Guide > Write and keep > Share, Export & Print > Export as MusicXML
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/export-as-musicxml/
Summary: MusicXML opens in MuseScore, Finale, Sibelius, Dorico, Notion and most other notation apps.

Press and hold a score in the library, choose **Share**, then **Export as MusicXML**. It opens in MuseScore, Finale, Sibelius, Dorico, Notion and most other notation apps. For a PDF score it is the music the app read from the page.
