# Rests mode

Path: Guide > Write and keep > Writing & Editing Music > Rests mode
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/editor-rests-mode/
Summary: Turn a note into a rest, change a rest's length, or take a rest off the page.

Tap a note to turn it into a rest of the chosen length. Tap a rest to select it and change its length, or use the bin to take it off the page (the bar keeps its length). The length buttons show the rest and, under it, how many beats it lasts (4, 2, 1, ½, ¼), which tells the whole rest from the half rest. In a new, empty bar you can touch anywhere along the bar to choose its rest.
