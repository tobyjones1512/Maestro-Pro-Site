# Turn pages and start playback with a pedal

Path: Guide > Start here > Playing a Score > Turn pages and start playback with a pedal
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/midi-pedals-and-page-turners/
Summary: Give a MIDI pedal a job in Settings, Listening; a Bluetooth page turner works with no setting up.

**A MIDI instrument's pedals**

1. Open **Settings**, **Listening**.
2. For **Sustain Pedal**, **Middle Pedal** and **Soft Pedal**, choose **Nothing**, **Play or Pause**, **Next Page**, **Previous Page** or **Go to Beginning**.

Pressing the pedal then does that while a score is open. Leave a pedal on Nothing if you use it for playing.

**A Bluetooth page turner**

Pair the pedal with your iPad or Mac in the device's Bluetooth settings. These pedals press keyboard keys, usually Page Up and Page Down or the arrow keys. Page Down and Page Up already turn the page; if your pedal sends other keys, give those keys to Next Page and Previous Page in [keyboard shortcuts](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/change-a-shortcut/).
