# Export as PDF

Path: Guide > Write and keep > Share, Export & Print > Export as PDF
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/export-as-pdf/
Summary: A PDF score is passed on as it is; any other score is typeset in the key currently shown.

**Export as PDF** gives you the printed page. A PDF score is passed on as it is; any other score is typeset in the key currently shown. Your annotations are included for the key and zoom you are viewing.
