# iPad requirements

Path: Guide > Your devices and privacy > Devices and compatibility > iPad requirements
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/ipad-requirements/
Summary: iPadOS 17 or later, covering iPad Pro, iPad Air, iPad and iPad mini generations listed here.

Maestro Pro needs iPadOS 17 or later. That covers:

- iPad Pro: 12.9-inch (2nd generation) and later, 11-inch (all), 10.5-inch
- iPad Air: 3rd generation and later
- iPad: 6th generation and later
- iPad mini: 5th generation and later
