# Reduce vocals on a track

Path: Guide > Sound and words > Backing Tracks & Sound > Reduce vocals on a track
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/reduce-vocals/
Summary: Reduce Vocals cuts the singer down in a stereo recording; it cannot remove a voice from a mono recording.

**Reduce Vocals**, on any track, cuts the singer down in a stereo recording by cancelling what is in the middle. It leaves a mono track, can take some middle instruments with it, and cannot remove a voice from a mono recording.
