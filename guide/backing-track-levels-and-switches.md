# Track switches and levels

Path: Guide > Sound and words > Backing Tracks & Sound > Track switches and levels
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/backing-track-levels-and-switches/
Summary: Each track has its own switch and its own level from minus 24 to plus 12 decibels.

Under **Backing Tracks & Sound** each track has its own switch, to turn it off without removing it, and its own level from -24 to +12 decibels.
