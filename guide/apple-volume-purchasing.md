# Apple volume purchasing

Path: Guide > Plans and licences > Education and schools > Apple volume purchasing
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/apple-volume-purchasing/
Summary: Schools can roll the free version out through Apple School Manager or Apple Business Manager.

Apple lets schools and organisations buy apps in bulk through **Apps and Books** in Apple School Manager or Apple Business Manager, both free to enrol and needing the organisation to be verified by Apple.

The free version of Maestro Pro can be rolled out to every device this way. Performer and Professional are subscriptions, and Apple's volume purchasing covers apps rather than subscriptions, so each person who needs a paid plan subscribes on their own Apple Account. Volume purchasing is not offered in every country or region.
