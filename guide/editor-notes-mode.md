# Notes mode: add and move notes

Path: Guide > Write and keep > Writing & Editing Music > Notes mode: add and move notes
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/editor-notes-mode/
Summary: Slide on a rest to choose a pitch, lift to place a note, drag to move one, scribble to rub it out.

Touch a rest and slide up or down to choose the pitch; lift to put a note there, as long as the length chosen below. Drag a note up or down to move it. Scribble back and forth over a note to rub it out. Select a note, then tap a length to change how long it lasts.
