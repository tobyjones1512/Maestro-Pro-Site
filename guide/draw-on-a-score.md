# Draw on a score

Path: Guide > Practise > Annotating > Draw on a score
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/draw-on-a-score/
Summary: Turn on Annotate in the toolbar and draw; the ink is saved with the score and shows while you play.

Turn on **Annotate** in the toolbar and draw with Apple Pencil using the tool palette. Ink is saved with the score and shows while you play. Annotation is part of the Performer and Professional plans.
