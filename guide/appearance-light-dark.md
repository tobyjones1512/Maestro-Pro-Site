# Light, dark or system appearance

Path: Guide > Settings and help > Settings & Accessibility > Light, dark or system appearance
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/appearance-light-dark/
Summary: Appearance is light, dark, or follows the system.

**Appearance** is light, dark or follow the system. Dark sheet music has a default in Settings which any score can override.
