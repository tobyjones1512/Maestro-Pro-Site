# Score toolbar icons (1 of 2)

Path: Guide > Settings and help > Buttons and icons > Score toolbar icons (1 of 2)
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/icons-score-toolbar-1/
Summary: What each icon does: Edit Score, Annotate, Wait for Me, Record Myself and more.

- {{icon:musicu2equarternoteu2e3}} **Edit Score**: opens the score editor.
- {{icon:pencilu2etipu2ecropu2ecircle}} **Annotate**: draw on the page with Apple Pencil.
- {{icon:earu2eandu2ewaveform}} **Wait for Me**: the music waits until it hears you play the next note.
- {{icon:recordu2ecircle}} **Record Myself**: records you with the microphone so you can review the take.
- {{icon:tuningfork}} **Tuner**: tells you how far in tune the note you play is.
- {{icon:slideru2ehorizontalu2e3}} **Inspector**: opens the panel with this score's settings: key, tempo, backing tracks, staves and appearance.
- {{icon:squareu2egridu2e2x2}} **Chord View**: shows the chords of the score as a grid.
- {{icon:quoteu2ebubble}} **Show Lyrics Instead of Music**: swaps the page for the words.
- {{icon:personu2e2u2ewaveu2e2}} **Play Together**: plays in time with other devices.
- {{icon:plusu2emagnifyingglass}} **Zoom In**: makes the music bigger.
- {{icon:minusu2emagnifyingglass}} **Zoom Out**: makes the music smaller.
- {{icon:arrowu2eupu2eleftu2eandu2earrowu2edownu2eright}} **Fit to Width**: sizes the page to fill the screen width.
