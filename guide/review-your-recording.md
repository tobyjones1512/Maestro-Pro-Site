# Review your take

Path: Guide > Practise > Record & Review > Review your take
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/review-your-recording/
Summary: The take is shown with the notes lit: green where you should have been, red where you actually were.

After recording, review the take with the notes lit: green where you should have been, red where you actually were. It listens through the microphone, so it works with any instrument or your voice, and it shows wrong notes, late entries and the bars to work on.
