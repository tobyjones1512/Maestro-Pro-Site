# Microphone permission

Path: Guide > Your devices and privacy > Privacy and permissions > Microphone permission
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/microphone-permission/
Summary: The microphone hears whether you are playing the right note, tunes, and records you, all on the device.

The microphone is used to hear whether you are playing the right note, to tune, and to record you. Audio is processed on the device. Recordings are saved only in your library. If the app cannot hear you, allow the microphone in the device's Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Microphone.
