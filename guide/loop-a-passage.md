# Loop a hard passage

Path: Guide > Start here > Playing a Score > Loop a hard passage
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/loop-a-passage/
Summary: In the Inspector, Loop plays the bars you choose over and over.

In the Inspector, **Loop** plays the bars you choose over and over, to practise a hard passage. Pair it with a slower [practice speed](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/change-practice-speed/) and speed it up again when the passage is ready.
