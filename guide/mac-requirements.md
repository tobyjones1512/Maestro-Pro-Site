# Mac requirements

Path: Guide > Your devices and privacy > Devices and compatibility > Mac requirements
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/mac-requirements/
Summary: macOS 14 Sonoma or later, with keyboard shortcuts, multiple windows and trackpad or mouse drawing.

Maestro Pro needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later. On a Mac you get the keyboard shortcuts, multiple windows and trackpad or mouse drawing.
