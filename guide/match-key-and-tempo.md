# Match Key and Tempo to a track

Path: Guide > Sound and words > Backing Tracks & Sound > Match Key and Tempo to a track
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/match-key-and-tempo/
Summary: Match Key & Tempo works on the first backing track; use it again if the track starts late or early.

**Match Key & Tempo** sets the score to the key and tempo of the first backing track. Typing a tempo or tapping it makes the tempo your own, and matching a backing track does too. If a track starts late or early, see [A backing track starts late or early](https://maestro-pro.app/guide/backing-track-starts-late-or-early/).
