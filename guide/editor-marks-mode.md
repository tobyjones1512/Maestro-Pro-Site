# Marks mode: accidentals, ties and articulations

Path: Guide > Write and keep > Writing & Editing Music > Marks mode: accidentals, ties and articulations
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/editor-marks-mode/
Summary: Add a sharp, flat or natural, tie notes, or give a note staccato, accent, tenuto, marcato or a fermata.

Tap a note, then add a sharp, flat or natural, tie it to the next note, or give it staccato, accent, tenuto, marcato or a fermata. Tap one again to take it off.
