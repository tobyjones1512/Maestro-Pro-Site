# Optional extras the app can use

Path: Guide > Your devices and privacy > Devices and compatibility > Optional extras the app can use
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/optional-extras/
Summary: Microphone, camera (planned), Apple Music, iCloud Drive, local network and the internet are all optional.

- Microphone for the tuner, Wait for Me and recording.
- Camera for scanning printed music, which is coming in a future update.
- Apple Music subscription to play along with a song and see its lyrics.
- iCloud Drive to keep your library on all your devices.
- Local network permission for Play Together.
- Internet to search for scores, lyrics and singers, and for GitHub.
