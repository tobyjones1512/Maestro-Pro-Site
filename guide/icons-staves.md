# Mute, solo and hide staff icons

Path: Guide > Settings and help > Buttons and icons > Mute, solo and hide staff icons
URL: https://maestro-pro.app/guide/icons-staves/
Summary: What each icon does: Mute, Solo, Hide Staff, Show Staff.

- {{icon:tu3aM}} **Mute**: silences a staff while the music plays.
- {{icon:tu3aS}} **Solo**: plays only the staves marked Solo.
- {{icon:eye}} **Hide Staff**: takes a staff off the page. It stays off in exported and printed PDFs, so you can print one part.
- {{icon:eyeu2eslash}} **Show Staff**: puts a hidden staff back.
