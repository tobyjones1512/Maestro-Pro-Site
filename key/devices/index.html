Your licence devices

A licence bought on this website works on up to 3 devices. Enter your licence key to see where yours is being used, and remove any device you no longer use. Removing a device frees its place straight away.

Don't have your key to hand? Have it emailed to you first.

Which device is which?

Maestro Pro checks the licence each time it is opened. To find a device, open the app on it, then press Refresh here: that device's last used time moves to the top. A removed device is signed out the next time it checks, and a device that has not been used for 60 days is removed automatically.

Education licences have no device limit.
