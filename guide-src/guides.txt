## what-is-maestro-pro | getting-started | What Maestro Pro is
summary: Maestro Pro is an app for musicians that reads your scores, follows them to the note and plays along.
keywords: what is, about, overview, features, app, sheet music
see: your-first-minute, plans-and-pricing, devices-and-compatibility

Maestro Pro is a working tool for musicians. It reads your scores and follows them to the note, turns pages for you, and rehearses with a backing track, a song from Apple Music, the built-in instrument or a metronome. It also lets you write and revise the music itself.

It runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac. You can slow a hard passage down without changing the pitch, transpose for your instrument, record yourself against the score, and play along with other devices.

## your-first-minute | getting-started | Your first minute
summary: Import a score, press Play and tap the music to start from any point.
keywords: first minute, start, begin, import score, play, tap to start, progress bar
see: import-a-score, open-the-sample-score, play-and-pause

1. Tap **Import Score** on the home screen (or the + button, then Import Score) and choose a PDF, MusicXML file or any other supported file.
2. The score appears in your library on the left. Tap it to open it.
3. Press the big **Play** button at the bottom. The music plays and follows along.

Tap anywhere on the music to start from there, or drag the progress bar.

## free-plan-limits | getting-started | What the Free plan includes
summary: The Free plan keeps up to three scores and lets you view and play them, with some tools left for paid plans.
keywords: free, free plan, limits, three scores, what is free, cannot, paywall
see: free-plan, compare-plans, performer-plan

On the Free plan you can keep up to three scores and view the sheet music for each. You can open lyrics files, play along with your own audio files, connect your own GitHub repositories and add tracks from them, and make scales and arpeggios in any key.

The built-in synth instruments play the score, but nothing follows along on the page: there is no note highlighting, bar highlighting or moving line until Performer. Nothing that needs the internet is included, and there is no equaliser, key changes or other tools.

## devices-per-licence | licences-and-keys | Use your licence on up to 3 devices
summary: A licence bought on the website works on up to 3 devices. Education licences have no limit.
keywords: devices, three devices, 3 devices, device limit, how many devices, another device, new iphone, new ipad, share licence, limit reached
see: remove-a-device, enter-your-licence-key, buy-for-your-organisation

A licence bought on the website works on up to **3 devices**: for example an iPhone, an iPad and a Mac. Enter the same key on each one. A device is counted the first time it is used with the key, and the key is checked each time the app is opened.

If you try a fourth device, Maestro Pro says the licence is already in use on 3 devices. [Remove a device](remove-a-device) you no longer use to free its place, then enter your key again.

A device that has not been used for 60 days stops counting automatically. If you delete the app and install it again, that counts as a new device until the old one is removed or ages out, so remove the old one if you reach the limit.

Education licences, bought for a whole organisation, have no device limit. See [Buy for a whole organisation](buy-for-your-organisation).

## remove-a-device | licences-and-keys | Remove a device from your licence
summary: Use the devices page on the key page to see where your licence is used and remove a device.
keywords: remove device, revoke device, sign out device, free a place, device limit reached, old iphone, sold device, lost device, devices page
see: devices-per-licence, recover-your-licence-key, licence-key-not-working

1. Open the [devices page](/key/devices/).
2. Enter your licence key and choose **Show my devices**.
3. Find the device you no longer use. If you are not sure which it is, open Maestro Pro on it and choose **Refresh**: the device you just used moves to the top with a new last used time.
4. Choose **Remove** next to the device you want to take off.

The place is free straight away, so you can enter your key on another device. A removed device is signed out the next time Maestro Pro checks the licence, which is each time the app is opened. You can use **Remove all devices** to start again.

If you do not have your key, [recover your licence key](/key.html) first.

## editor-bars-mode | writing-editing-music | Bars mode: repeats, adding and deleting bars
summary: Repeat signs, adding a bar before or after, deleting one or several bars, and Expand Repeats to write a piece out as it is played.

Tap a bar to choose it. **Start** and **End** put repeat signs on it (End cycles through two, three and four times). **Add Bar Before** and **Add Bar After** put an empty bar, the same length and resting in every staff, next to it. ## when-the-app-uses-the-internet | privacy-and-permissions | When the app uses the internet
summary: Only when you ask it to: searching for scores, lyrics or singers, downloading a score, and GitHub.
keywords: internet, online, offline, network use, search internet, does it need wifi
see: find-free-scores, find-lyrics-online, connect-github

The app uses the internet only when you ask it to. Searching for scores, lyrics or singers sends your search words (and, to match singers, the song title and artist) to the services that answer: the web search engine used for Search Scores and the sites it finds, GitHub, LRCLIB (lyrics) and Genius (singers). Downloading a free score fetches it from the site that hosts it. Those services have their own privacy policies.

## usage-statistics | privacy-and-permissions | Anonymous usage statistics
summary: Maestro Pro counts which features are used with Aptabase, with no account, name, email or fixed device identifier.
keywords: analytics, aptabase, usage statistics, tracking, telemetry, anonymous
see: privacy-and-permissions

Maestro Pro uses Aptabase, a privacy-friendly analytics service hosted in the EU, to count how the app is used. Each event is a short name, such as "play" or "export_pdf", with a few simple details such as a file type or an on/off choice. There is no account, no name, no email address, no advertising identifier and no fixed device identifier, so events cannot be tied back to you. Nothing from your library is ever sent: no score or song titles, lyrics, search words, file names, recordings or drawings.
