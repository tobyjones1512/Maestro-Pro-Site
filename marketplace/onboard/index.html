Become a seller

Sell your sheet music, backing tracks and karaoke to Maestro Pro players. You keep 70% of every sale and Maestro Pro keeps 30%. You set your own price in whole US dollars, and buyers are charged in their own currency.

Payments are handled by Mollie. You will connect (or create) a Mollie account in the next step, and Mollie pays your share out to you.
