Dispute Resolution Centre

If something on the Marketplace uses your work without your permission, tell us here. The item goes off sale while we look at it, the merchant is asked to respond, and a person at Maestro Pro decides. If the dispute is upheld the item is removed for good and the merchant gets a copyright strike. Three strikes close their account. Read the rules.
