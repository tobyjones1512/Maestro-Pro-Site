Dispute Resolution Centre

Copyright disputes about items on the Marketplace. A rights holder can file a dispute. An item is off sale while a dispute is open, and you have 7 days to respond. Three upheld disputes close a Merchant Account (guideline 8).

Escalations

Items refused three times. Your decision is final: accept it, or refuse it permanently.

Appeal:

Explain why this item should be accepted and add evidence such as a licence, a letter of permission or proof that you wrote it. Every file is scanned with VirusTotal, then the item is assessed again. After three refusals a person makes the final decision.
